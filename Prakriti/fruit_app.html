What are Fruits?

Fruits are the edible parts of flowering plants that contain seeds. They come in many colors, shapes and flavors. Fruits are naturally sweet and packed with important nutrients.

Why Do We Eat Fruits?

We eat fruits because they provide vitamins, minerals, fiber and water. They help us stay healthy, energetic and hydrated.

Why Are Fruits Important?

Fruits support healthy growth, improve digestion, strengthen immunity, promote healthy skin and reduce the risk of many diseases. Eating a variety of fruits every day helps maintain a balanced diet.

FAQ

Q: How many fruits should I eat daily?

A: 2–4 servings are generally recommended.

Q: Which fruit contains Vitamin C?

A: Oranges, kiwi and strawberries are rich in Vitamin C.

Q: Are fruits healthy snacks?

A: Yes, fruits are nutritious and naturally sweet.
